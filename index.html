AbaWeather

Simple weather, without the clutter.

AbaWeather is a personal weather app built for friends and family, with current conditions, forecasts, precipitation chances, severe weather alerts, and plain-language weather summaries.

Support

If you have a question, found a bug, or need help using AbaWeather, email abaweather@outlook.com.

Weather data

AbaWeather uses Apple WeatherKit to provide weather information. The app may use your device location, with your permission, to show weather for your current area. You can also search for another location.

Privacy

AbaWeather does not use advertising or third-party tracking. For more information about how location and other data are handled, see the Privacy Policy.

Important notice

This application is a personal weather project developed for friends and family. Weather information is provided for convenience only and should not be relied upon for life-safety decisions.

Always follow instructions and warnings from local authorities and official emergency-management agencies.
