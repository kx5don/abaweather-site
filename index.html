AbaWeather

Simple weather, without the clutter.

AbaWeather is a personal weather app built for friends and family. It combines current conditions, short-term and extended forecasts, severe weather alerts, animated radar, location search, an iPhone/iPad widget, and concise plain-language AbaCast weather summaries.

Support

If you have a question, found a bug, or need help using AbaWeather, email abaweather@outlook.com.

Weather data

AbaWeather uses Tomorrow.io for current conditions. U.S. National Weather Service (NWS) data is used for forecasts, severe weather alerts, radar imagery, and as a fallback source for current observations when needed.

AbaCast uses the weather already displayed by AbaWeather to generate a short plain-language summary. AbaCast requests are processed through the AbaWeather backend and OpenAI.

Locations and maps

With your permission, AbaWeather can use your device location to show weather for your current area. You can also search for another location. Apple mapping and geocoding services are used for location search and map display.

Privacy

AbaWeather does not use advertising or behavioral tracking. For more information about how location, weather requests, and AbaCast data are handled, see the Privacy Policy.

Important notice

This application is a personal weather project developed for friends and family. Weather information is provided for convenience only and should not be relied upon for life-safety decisions.

Always follow instructions and warnings from local authorities and official emergency-management agencies.
