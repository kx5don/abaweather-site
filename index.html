Simple weather, without the clutter.

AbaWeather is a personal weather app built for friends and family, designed to make it easy to understand what the weather is doing now, what is coming next, and whether anything needs your attention.

Support

If you have a question, found a bug, or need help using AbaWeather, email abaweather@outlook.com.

What AbaWeather includes

AbaWeather provides current conditions, short-term and extended forecasts, severe weather alerts, animated weather radar, location search, an iPhone and iPad widget, and AbaCast — a concise plain-language summary of the weather ahead.

The app is intentionally focused on the weather information most people need day to day, without the advertising, news feeds, and extra clutter common in many weather apps.

Weather information

AbaWeather combines multiple trusted weather sources to provide current conditions, forecasts, alerts, and radar information. Because weather observations and forecasts can change quickly, the information shown in the app may update throughout the day.

Privacy

AbaWeather does not use advertising or behavioral tracking. Details about location information, weather requests, AbaCast, and other data handling are available in the Privacy Policy.

Important notice

This application is a personal weather project developed for friends and family. Weather information is provided for convenience only and should not be relied upon for life-safety decisions.

Always follow instructions and warnings from local authorities and official emergency-management agencies.
