Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Contact: abaweather@outlook.com

1. Information We Collect

AbaWeather is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary. The app does not require you to create an account.

Location information. If you allow location access, AbaWeather may receive your device's current location. If you search for another location, AbaWeather receives the location you select or enter.

Weather notification information. If you enable weather notifications, AbaWeather collects an identifier associated with your app installation, the location associated with your notification registration, and limited records of weather alerts previously delivered to your device.

AbaCast information. Information associated with an AbaCast request may include weather observations, forecast information, and a general location name such as a city or area. Raw GPS coordinates are not included in the information provided to the AI service for AbaCast.

Information stored on your device. AbaWeather may store preferences, recently retrieved weather information, and other app information locally on your device.

AbaWeather does not collect information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or cross-app tracking and does not include third-party advertising or behavioral-tracking technology.

2. How We Use Information

AbaWeather uses the information described above only to provide, maintain, protect, and improve the app and its features. In particular, AbaWeather uses information to:

  • Determine which weather conditions, forecasts, alerts, radar, and maps are relevant to the location you are viewing.
  • Monitor for weather alerts, deliver push notifications you have enabled, and avoid repeatedly sending the same alert.
  • Generate AbaCast's short, plain-language weather summaries.
  • Remember app preferences and support normal app operation.
  • Improve performance and reliability and help prevent abuse or misuse of the service.

AbaWeather does not sell or rent personal information and does not use your information to deliver targeted advertising or track you across unrelated apps or websites.

3. Service Providers

AbaWeather uses outside services to provide some app features. Those providers may receive information necessary to perform the service being requested.

Tomorrow.io provides current weather information.

U.S. National Weather Service (NWS) services provide forecasts, weather alerts, radar information, and other weather data.

Mapbox provides mapping services. Mapbox may receive information about the geographic area being viewed and other information normally associated with providing online map content.

OpenAI provides the artificial intelligence service used to generate AbaCast summaries.

Apple provides services used to deliver push notifications to Apple devices.

Cloud service providers provide infrastructure used to operate AbaWeather and its online services.

These providers process information according to their own privacy practices and terms. AbaWeather expects service providers that handle information on its behalf to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable privacy requirements.

4. Retention and Deletion

AbaWeather keeps information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Weather information used for ordinary app requests may be retained temporarily to improve performance and reduce unnecessary requests to weather providers. Records of previously delivered weather alerts are also retained temporarily and then deleted.

If you use weather notifications, information associated with your notification registration may be retained as necessary to provide that service. You may request deletion of information associated with your use of AbaWeather by contacting abaweather@outlook.com.

Information stored locally by AbaWeather on your device is generally removed when the app and its associated data are deleted from the device.

AbaWeather uses reasonable safeguards designed to protect information processed through the service, but no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You control whether AbaWeather can access your device's location and whether it can send you notifications. You can change these permissions at any time through your device's settings. If you choose not to provide device location access, you can still search for and view weather for a location you select.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights concerning personal information associated with you, including rights to request access, correction, or deletion.

Because AbaWeather does not use personal accounts, some information may be associated only with an app installation or device rather than your name or email address. If you would like to exercise a privacy right, request deletion, or ask a privacy question, contact abaweather@outlook.com. AbaWeather may need enough information to identify the relevant app installation or data before completing a request.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as AbaWeather's features, service providers, or privacy practices change. If material changes are made, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
