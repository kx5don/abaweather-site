AbaWeather

Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Overview

AbaWeather is a personal weather application developed for friends and family. It is designed to provide weather information without advertising, behavioral profiling, or third-party tracking.

Location information

If you grant location permission, AbaWeather uses your device location to determine the area for which weather information should be displayed. Location access is used only to provide the app's weather features.

AbaWeather does not maintain a developer-operated database of your location history and does not sell, rent, or use location information for advertising or tracking.

You can deny or revoke location access at any time through iOS Settings. Location access is required for AbaWeather to provide weather information for your current location.

Weather services

AbaWeather retrieves weather information from Apple WeatherKit and from public-domain data provided by the U.S. National Weather Service (NWS). Requests to Apple services are subject to Apple's applicable privacy practices and terms. NWS information is supplied by the U.S. government for public use.

Data collection and tracking

AbaWeather does not include third-party advertising, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies. The developer does not use the app to create advertising profiles or sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

The app may store limited preferences or app state locally on your device as necessary for normal operation. This information is not used for advertising or tracking.

Children

AbaWeather is a general-purpose weather application and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The developer does not knowingly operate a service that profiles or markets to children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if AbaWeather's features or data practices change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about AbaWeather or this Privacy Policy can be sent to abaweather@outlook.com.
